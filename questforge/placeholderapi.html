Plugins / QuestForge

PlaceholderAPI

PlaceholderAPI

With PlaceholderAPI installed, QuestForge registers its placeholders on startup. No /papi ecloud download is needed.

Placeholder Returns
%questforge_active_count% Number of quests the player has in progress
%questforge_completed_count% Number of quests the player has completed
%questforge_current_quest_name% Name of the tracked quest, or None
%questforge_current_objective% Current objective of the tracked quest with its progress, or None

A player tracks a quest with /quest track <id> or from the quest log.

Example for a scoreboard plugin:

lines:
  - "&7Quests done: &a%questforge_completed_count%"
  - "&7Now: &e%questforge_current_objective%"
