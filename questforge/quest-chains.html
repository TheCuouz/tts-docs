Plugins / QuestForge

Quest chains

A quest with prerequisites stays locked until the player has completed every quest in the list. That is all a chain is.

id: apprentice
name: "<gray>Apprentice"
objectives:
  - type: KILL
    entity: ZOMBIE
    amount: 5

id: journeyman
name: "<yellow>Journeyman"
prerequisites:
  - apprentice
objectives:
  - type: KILL
    entity: SKELETON
    amount: 10

id: master
name: "<gold>Master"
prerequisites:
  - journeyman
  - side_quest_1
objectives:
  - type: KILL
    entity: WITHER_SKELETON
    amount: 3

master needs both journeyman and side_quest_1. Branches work the same way: two paths that end in one quest listing both as prerequisites.

When a player finishes the last prerequisite, the next quest shows up in their /quest log as available. Trying to start a locked quest tells them the prerequisites are not done yet.
