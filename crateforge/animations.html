Plugins / CrateForge

Animations

CrateForge includes 11 unique animations that play when a crate is opened. Each animation provides a different visual experience for players. Set the animation per-crate in the crate's .yml file.

Quick reference

Animation Style Best For
CSGO Horizontal scrolling chest Classic, familiar to players
ROULETTE Spinning wheel of prizes Casino feel
WHEEL Large rotating prize wheel Spectacle, big moments
QUAD 4-panel simultaneous reveal Fast-paced, exciting
SLOT 3-reel slot machine Casino/gambling servers
COSMIC Particle burst, cosmic reveal Prestige/legendary crates
WAR Battle-style reveal PvP servers
FIREWORKS Fireworks + prize reveal Celebratory feel
CASCADE Waterfall prize drop Unique, visually interesting
AUCTION Bidding-style countdown Economy servers
MYSTERY Suspense-building hidden reveal Mystery/Halloween events

Animation descriptions

CSGO

"The classic Counter-Strike case-opening experience."

A horizontal scrolling display of prizes, gradually decelerating until it lands on the winning prize. Familiar to most players from CS:GO/CS2 case openings.

  • Style: Horizontal item scroll
  • Duration: ~5-8 seconds
  • Player feel: Suspenseful, rising tension as items slow down
  • Best for: General use, familiar and intuitive for all players
animation: CSGO

ROULETTE

"Place your bets — the wheel decides."

A classic roulette-style spinning wheel that gradually slows and lands on the winning segment. Items are arranged around the wheel.

  • Style: Circular spinning wheel
  • Duration: ~6-10 seconds
  • Player feel: Casino atmosphere, suspenseful
  • Best for: Casino/gambling-themed servers
animation: ROULETTE

WHEEL

"The grand wheel spins before the crowd."

A larger, more dramatic version of the roulette — a full rotating prize wheel with a visual pointer indicating the winner.

  • Style: Large rotating prize wheel with pointer
  • Duration: ~8-12 seconds
  • Player feel: Grand, show-stopping
  • Best for: Special events, legendary crates, watching in groups
animation: WHEEL

QUAD

"Four panels flip simultaneously."

Four item slots are revealed simultaneously in a grid layout, each cycling through prizes before stopping one by one.

  • Style: 4-panel grid flip
  • Duration: ~4-6 seconds
  • Player feel: Fast-paced, multi-reward feel
  • Best for: Common/frequent crates, kit servers
animation: QUAD

SLOT

"Pull the lever. Match the symbols."

A 3-reel slot machine animation. Each reel spins and stops independently, left to right, until the prize is revealed.

  • Style: 3-reel slot machine
  • Duration: ~5-8 seconds
  • Player feel: Classic gambling, exciting near-misses
  • Best for: Casino servers, economy servers
animation: SLOT

COSMIC

"Your prize emerges from the cosmos."

A dramatic particle-burst animation with cosmic/space visual effects surrounding the prize reveal.

  • Style: Particle burst + cosmic particle effects
  • Duration: ~6-10 seconds
  • Player feel: Prestigious, otherworldly
  • Best for: Legendary/prestige crates, special event crates
animation: COSMIC

WAR

"Battle-hardened rewards for the victorious."

A military/battle-themed reveal with dramatic sound effects and combat visual effects.

  • Style: Battle-themed reveal
  • Duration: ~5-8 seconds
  • Player feel: Intense, aggressive
  • Best for: PvP servers, war-themed servers, Kit PvP
animation: WAR

FIREWORKS

"Celebrate your victory with fireworks!"

Fireworks explode around the player as the prize is revealed, creating a celebratory atmosphere.

  • Style: Fireworks display + prize reveal
  • Duration: ~6-10 seconds
  • Player feel: Celebratory, exciting, festive
  • Best for: Event crates, holiday crates, milestone rewards
animation: FIREWORKS

CASCADE

"Prizes waterfall down until yours remains."

Prizes cascade downward like a waterfall, with items disappearing one by one until only the winner remains.

  • Style: Waterfall/cascade of items
  • Duration: ~6-8 seconds
  • Player feel: Unique and satisfying, visually distinct
  • Best for: Servers wanting something different from the standard scroll
animation: CASCADE

AUCTION

"The bidding war decides your fate."

A countdown/auction-style animation where prize values count down until the winning prize is revealed at the final moment.

  • Style: Auction countdown reveal
  • Duration: ~8-12 seconds
  • Player feel: Tense, economy-minded
  • Best for: Economy servers, auction-house themed servers
animation: AUCTION

MYSTERY

"You won't know what you got until the last second."

A suspense-building animation where the prize is hidden behind question marks or mystery icons until the final dramatic reveal.

  • Style: Hidden mystery reveal with suspense buildup
  • Duration: ~8-12 seconds
  • Player feel: Maximum suspense, Halloween/mystery themed
  • Best for: Mystery boxes, Halloween events, surprise crates
animation: MYSTERY

Choosing the right animation

Server Type Recommended Animations
Survival / SMP CSGO, ROULETTE, FIREWORKS
PvP / Kit PvP WAR, CSGO, QUAD
Economy / Casino SLOT, ROULETTE, AUCTION
RPG / Fantasy COSMIC, MYSTERY, WHEEL
Event / Holiday FIREWORKS, MYSTERY, WHEEL
Prestigious/Rare COSMIC, WHEEL, AUCTION
