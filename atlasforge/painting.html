Plugins / AtlasForge

Painting

Paint where you say

/atlas paint <pattern> [mask…] [--seed n]

Paints the selection with a block, a mix or a pattern, only where every mask agrees.

/atlas paint moss_block #flat #near[water,3] #height[-64,70]
/atlas paint #noise[cellular,20,3,stone,1,sand]

FAWE-style patterns such as #vor[5][stone,sand] work too.

#flat, #hillside, #cliff and #slope[min,max] keep to the ground you can see: the top of each column and the faces of steps and cliffs. Add #depth[0,3] to reach that far under it.

Gradients

/atlas gradient <blocks> [mask…] [--by y|x|z|radial|sphere|depth]

Lays blocks in bands. Join two blocks with .. and the blocks in between are found by colour:

/atlas gradient stone..deepslate --blend noise
/atlas gradient --palette meadow --by radial

--steps sets how many blocks to find between each pair joined with .. (0 keeps only the blocks you wrote; if fewer blocks sit on that colour line the ramp comes out shorter and it says so), --blend is none, random or noise, --width is how far the bands fray into each other, --reverse runs it the other way, and --show only lists the blocks it would use.

Texture

/atlas texture <palette> [scale] [seed]

The layered recipe builders use by hand: blobs of the main blocks, darker edges and a speckle on top.

Palettes

/atlas palette                      list them
/atlas palette <name>               the blocks in one, and whether they hold together
/atlas palette check <a> <b>        do these two blocks read apart, and from how far

Brushes

/atlas brush <type> [--size n] [--block b] [mask…]

Puts a brush in your hand; right click paints. Types: blob, boulder, sphere, cylinder, cube, smooth, erode, scatter, draw, layer, tube, branch, roots.

/atlas brush scatter --size 6 --block poppy,short_grass,oxeye_daisy --density 0.3
/atlas brush tube --size 4 --height 12 --block stone_bricks
/atlas brush branch --block oak_log --length 14
/atlas brush layer --block snow --on anything
/atlas brush draw --block dirt_path --size 1
