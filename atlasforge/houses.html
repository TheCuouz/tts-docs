Plugins / AtlasForge

Houses, roofs and pieces

A house

/atlas house [--size small|medium|large] [--culture c] [--form f] [--state worn|ruined]

Invents a house and builds it where you stand: rooms with doors between them, windows, stairs or a ladder, a chimney that comes out through the roof, and furniture that fits each room (kitchen downstairs, bedrooms upstairs). No room is left without a light.

Option Values
--size small, medium, large
--width, --length 5 to 64, instead of a size
--floors 1 to 8
--form box, l, t, l-front, shed, lean-to, u, l-shed, tower
--facing north, east, south, west: where the door looks
--culture, --seed, --state as for a town
--preview shows the house as a glass volume first; /atlas confirm builds it, /atlas cancel drops it

A roof on walls you built

/atlas roof <shape> <block> [pitch]

Select the building with the WorldEdit wand. The command finds the walls and roofs them. Shapes are gable, hip, pyramid and flat; pitch runs from 0 to 4, and 1 is an ordinary roof.

Pieces

/atlas piece                         list them
/atlas piece <name> [culture]        place one where you stand
/atlas piece gallery <culture>       lay every piece out in a row

Lamps, wells, fountains, stalls, benches, carts, planters, and the indoor furniture. A piece is made of the blocks of the culture you give it.

Cultures

A culture is a YAML file: which block each part of a building is made of, roof shapes and pitch, facade features, and how yards are kept. The bundled ones are medieval, nordic, japanese, alpine, mediterranean, desert, stone and ruins.

To make your own, copy one into plugins/AtlasForge/cultures/, rename it and change what you like. base: names a culture to start from, so the file only has to say what differs.

/atlas culture                 list them
/atlas culture <name>          what one is made of
/atlas culture check <name>    what is wrong with a file
/atlas culture reload          pick up changes without a restart
