Permissions

Every node below is declared in the plugin itself, so your permissions plugin lists them without you typing them by hand.

NodeGrantsDefault
itemforge.useUse /item at all.operators
itemforge.nameRename the item in hand.operators
itemforge.loreEdit the lore.operators

itemforge.flagToggle item flags.operators
itemforge.unbreakableMake an item unbreakable.operators
itemforge.amountChange the stack size.operators
itemforge.modelSet custom model data.operators
itemforge.headTurn a head into somebody's head.operators
itemforge.reloadReload config and language files.operators
itemforge.format.colourUse colours in names and lore.operators

itemforge.limits.bypassIgnore the length and enchantment-level limits.nobody
