Commands and permissions

Commands

CommandWhat it doesAliases
/tradeTrade items and money with another player.
/trade <player> | accept | deny | cancel | log		/tradeforge, /tf

Permissions

Every node below is declared in the plugin itself, so your permissions plugin lists them without you typing them by hand.

NodeGrantsDefault
tradeforge.useSend and accept trade requests.everyone
tradeforge.moneyPut money on the table (needs Vault).everyone
tradeforge.logRead the trade log with /trade log.operators
tradeforge.reloadReload config and language files.operators
tradeforge.bypass.blockedTrade materials listed in trade.blocked-materials.operators
tradeforge.bypass.distanceTrade regardless of the distance limit.operators
