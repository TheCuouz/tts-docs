Plugins / EnchantsForge

Enchantments

EnchantsForge ships with 8 unique enchantments across combat, utility, and mobility categories. All enchantments are backed by PDC (PersistentDataContainer) — no fragile lore parsing.

Renamed: EnchantsForge was previously released as "CustomEnchants". The enchantments, levels, and mechanics below are unchanged.

Enchantment overview

Combat enchantments

Lifesteal

"Drain the life of your enemies to fuel your own."

Property Value
ID lifesteal
Max Level III
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Melee hit
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Heal Amount
I 1 (0.5 HP) per hit
II 2 (1.0 HP) per hit
III 4 (2.0 HP) per hit

Tip: Combines with Vampiric for sustained healing in both combat and kill scenarios.

Vampiric

"Draw power from the death of your foes."

Property Value
ID vampiric
Max Level II
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Kill
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Heal Amount
I 4 (2.0 HP) per kill
II 8 (4.0 HP) per kill

Note: Only triggers on player and hostile mob kills. Does not trigger on passive mobs.

Beheading

"Take their head as a trophy."

Property Value
ID beheading
Max Level III
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Kill
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Drop Chance
I 5% chance
II 10% chance
III 20% chance

Drops the appropriate head item — player skulls with correct skin for players, mob heads for skeletons/creepers/etc.

Sapper

"Hunger is the greatest weapon."

Property Value
ID sapper
Max Level II
Applies To Sword
Trigger Melee hit
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Hunger Drained
I 2 hunger points per hit
II 4 hunger points per hit

Tip: Very effective in prolonged fights — a starving player cannot sprint or regenerate health.

Thunderstruck

"Call down the wrath of the storm."

Property Value
ID thunderstruck
Max Level II
Applies To Sword
Trigger Melee hit (random chance)
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Lightning Chance
I 10% chance to strike lightning on hit
II 20% chance to strike lightning on hit

Warning: Lightning strikes deal damage and can ignite nearby flammable blocks. Use caution in wooden structures.

Mobility enchantments

Wings

"Defy gravity with the power of enchanted wings."

Property Value
ID wings
Max Level III
Applies To Chestplate
Trigger Passive (tick-based)
Conflicts —

Effect by Level:

Level Effect
I Double-jump in the air (1 mid-air jump)
II Double-jump with slight upward boost
III Double-jump with strong upward boost

Note: Wings uses the tick system — it runs on a BukkitRunnable loop. There is a brief cooldown between jumps to prevent flight exploits.

Utility enchantments

Excavator

"Mine entire areas with a single swing."

Property Value
ID excavator
Max Level III
Applies To Pickaxe, Shovel, Axe
Trigger Block break
Conflicts Telekinesis (can coexist)

Effect by Level:

Level Mining Area
I 3×3 area mining
II 3×3 area mining (faster)
III 3×3 area mining (maximum speed)

Tip: Sneak while mining to temporarily disable Excavator and mine single blocks.

Telekinesis

"Items fly to your hand before they hit the ground."

Property Value
ID telekinesis
Max Level I
Applies To Pickaxe, Shovel
Trigger Block break drop
Conflicts —

Effect:

All drops from broken blocks are sent directly to the player's inventory, bypassing the ground entirely. If the inventory is full, drops fall normally.

Note: Works with Excavator for mass mining with automatic item collection.

Conflict system
