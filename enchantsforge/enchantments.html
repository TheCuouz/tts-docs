Enchantments

Enchantments

EFV2Addon (formerly CustomEnchantsV2) adds 15 new enchantments across four categories, plus 2 exclusive fusion-only outputs that can only be obtained through the Fusion Altar. EFV2Addon is the official addon for the EnchantsForge base plugin.

Combat Enchantments

Berserker

"The lower your health, the more dangerous you become."

Property Value
ID berserker
Max Level II
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Melee hit

Effect by Level:

Level Damage Bonus (below 30% HP)
I +20% damage
II +35% damage

Tip: Pairs extremely well with Lifesteal from the EnchantsForge base plugin — you get more damage when low and heal back up.

Cleave

"Your strikes send shockwaves through groups of enemies."

Property Value
ID cleave
Max Level II
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Melee hit

Effect by Level:

Level AoE Damage Splash
I 40% splash damage to nearby enemies
II 60% splash damage to nearby enemies

Withering

"Leave your enemies to wither and decay."

Property Value
ID withering
Max Level II
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Melee hit

Effect by Level:

Level Effect
I Apply WITHER II for 3 seconds
II Apply WITHER II for 5 seconds

Headhunter

"One chance. One head."

Property Value
ID headhunter
Max Level I
Applies To Sword, Axe
Trigger Kill

Effect: 5% chance to drop the player's or mob's head on kill.

Note: Similar to the EnchantsForge Beheading enchant, but this is an EFV2Addon variant with a flat 5% rate at level I.

Reflective

"Pain is a mirror — what you send returns to you."

Property Value
ID reflective
Max Level III
Applies To Chestplate
Trigger Taking damage

Effect by Level:

Level Damage Reflected (capped at 50%)
I 15% reflected
II 30% reflected
III 45% reflected

Warning: Reflected damage is capped at 50% of incoming damage regardless of level to prevent feedback loops.

Ranged Enchantments

Explosive

"Every arrow is a small grenade."

Property Value
ID explosive
Max Level I
Applies To Bow, Crossbow
Trigger Arrow hit

Effect: Arrows create a cosmetic explosion on impact with 3-block AoE damage to nearby entities.

Note: The explosion is cosmetic — it does not destroy blocks or ignite terrain.

Homing

"Aim in the general direction. Let the arrow handle the rest."

Property Value
ID homing
Max Level II
Applies To Bow, Crossbow
Trigger Arrow in flight

Effect by Level:

Level Tracking Duration
I Arrow steers toward nearest enemy every 2 ticks for 3 seconds
II Arrow steers toward nearest enemy every 2 ticks for 5 seconds

Thor's Plus

"The god of thunder, in ranged form."

Property Value
ID thors_plus
Max Level II
Applies To Bow, Crossbow
Trigger Arrow hit on entity

Effect: Arrow hit strikes lightning at the target's location. 5-second cooldown per arrow.

Defensive Enchantments

Reinforced

"Forged in the deepest fires, your armor becomes unbreakable."

Property Value
ID reinforced
Max Level III
Applies To Any armor piece
Trigger Taking damage

Effect by Level:

Level Damage Reduction
I −4% all incoming damage
II −8% all incoming damage
III −12% all incoming damage

Soulbound

"This item refuses to leave your side — even in death."

Property Value
ID soulbound
Max Level I
Applies To Any item
Trigger Player death

Effect: The item is kept in inventory on death instead of dropping. 60-second anti-dupe protection after death.

Warning: The 60-second anti-dupe protection prevents players from giving the item to another player and dying again to duplicate it.

Death Cry

"Not today."

Property Value
ID death_cry
Max Level I
Applies To Any armor piece
Trigger Taking a killing blow

Effect: 30% chance to negate a killing blow entirely, leaving the player at 1 HP. 60-second cooldown.

Fusion-Only Enchantments

These enchantments cannot be obtained from the Enchanting Altar or /enchant give — they can only be created at the Fusion Altar.

Vampire Lord

Property Value
ID vampire_lord
Fusion Recipe Lifesteal III + Vampiric II
Max Level I
Applies To Sword, Axe

Effect: Combines Lifesteal (heal on hit) AND Vampiric (heal on kill) into a single enchant slot. The strongest healing enchantment in the plugin.

Excavator Master

Property Value
ID excavator_master
Fusion Recipe Excavator I + Telekinesis I
Max Level I
Applies To Pickaxe, Shovel, Axe

Effect: Combines Excavator (3×3 mining) AND Telekinesis (drops to inventory) into a single enchant slot. Mine massive areas with automatic item collection.
