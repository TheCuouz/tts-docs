Plugins / EnchantsForge

Enchantments

EnchantsForge ships with 32 enchantments — 15 of them new in 1.6 (marked ). They are stored in the item's data (PDC), not parsed from lore, so renaming an item or changing the language never loses them. Any of them can be switched off with disabled-enchants in config.yml (or by clicking it in /enchant browse).
