Permissions

Every node below is declared in the plugin itself, so your permissions plugin lists them without you typing them by hand.

NodeGrantsDefault
dominionforge.useUse the basic DominionForge commands (/df info, /df gui, /df chat, ...).everyone
dominionforge.createFound a faction with /df create.everyone
dominionforge.adminAdmin commands (/df reload, /df adminconfig, /df adminzone, /df adminpower, /df import, /df mapsync, /df bench).operators
dominionforge.admin.bypassBypass claim, build and protection restrictions.operators
dominionforge.anticheat.notifyBe told when someone goes over the block-break limit of a raid.operators
dominionforge.anticheat.seeinvisKeep seeing players who are inside an invisibility region.operators
dominionforge.raidguard.phaseWalk through full blocks without being sent back.operators
dominionforge.raidguard.exploitsSkip the nether roof, crop dupe, item dupe and book limits.operators
dominionforge.raidguard.pearlThrow pearls with no cooldown and into any land.operators
dominionforge.raidguard.lootPick up loot protected for whoever made the kill.operators
dominionforge.cannon.giveHand out TNT wands and sand bots (/df tntwand, /df sandbot).operators
dominionforge.cannon.fill.freeFill dispensers with /df tntfill without paying the TNT.operators
dominionforge.chunkbuster.giveHand out chunkbusters (/df chunkbuster).operators
dominionforge.chunkbuster.bypassUse a chunkbuster with no faction, no rank and no cooldown.operators
dominionforge.build.giveHand out sell wands and crop hoppers (/df sellwand, /df crophopper).operators
dominionforge.build.craft.bypassCraft what the denied-crafts list forbids.operators
dominionforge.printer.bypassUse the printer outside your own faction's land.operators
dominionforge.koth.adminStart and stop KOTH events (/df koth start, /df koth stop).operators
dominionforge.admin.expireGive an item a deadline with /df expire.operators
dominionforge.rename.protectedRename the materials listed in items.rename-protected.operators

dominionforge.commands.warmup.damageA warmup does not break when this player is hit.operators

dominionforge.admin.enderchestOpen somebody else's ender chest (/df enderchest <player>).operators
